Import rules

• Incoming: room and other money received.
• Outgoing: money paid to HA, drivers, people, gas suppliers or other recipients.
• Payment method: Cash / Online (never HA).
• Recipient: HA, person, car, supplier, etc.
• Accounting: mark transfers or advances separately from expenses.
• Ignore: manager's cash-in-hand and other manager-held balances.
• Duplicate import IDs are skipped. Please verify handwritten figures before importing.
